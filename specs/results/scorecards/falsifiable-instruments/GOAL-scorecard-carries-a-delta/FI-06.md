## The target cannot detect drift, only a jump

```
PA-06 -> v1     signed sum   -7 over 30 cells     8 of 9 movements NEGATIVE
PA-06 -> v2     signed sum   -5 over 30 cells     5 of 5 NEGATIVE
RERUN -> PA-06  signed sum  +13 over 20 cells     9 of 9 POSITIVE
```

The movement is **directional, not noise**, and `≤ 1 per judge` is a **max**
statistic blind to a consistent one-directional walk. `EVAL-RERUN → PA-06 →
FI-03` is an up-then-down excursion in which every round passes a per-round test
while the `total` column moves **4 of 20 points — 20% of the scale — on
byte-identical code** (`U`-p2: 11 → 15). **Four more rounds of "MET at −1" walks
D5 from 4 to 0 with every step certified within target.**

**And the framing itself is the finding.** MET against one sealed baseline and
MISSED against another, over the same bytes, means the delta is not a property of
the pair of artifacts — **it is a property of the pair of reading sessions.**
That falsifies *"the metric is the delta"* more directly than either verdict
does.

*FI-03 did not bury the miss.* Both its `RESULT.md` and the ledger open with
`MISSED`, and its REJECTED section names reporting the adjacent comparison alone
as a move it declined. That is better practice than the audit assumed and is
recorded as such.
