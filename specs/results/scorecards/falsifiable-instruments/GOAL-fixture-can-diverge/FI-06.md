## Six results on whether the replacement entails its own answer

**(a) `arm B SURVIVED` was the only possible outcome for ANY fault at that
site.** `arm_b_fake.py` composes `Ledger(quotas, InMemoryJournal())`;
`FileJournal` is never constructed under `:fake`; `FI-M16` is seeded in
`file_journal.py`. Four maximally destructive probes at the same site — read-back
returns nothing, read-back raises, append writes nothing, and **the constructor
raising unconditionally** — all give `KILLED KILLED SURVIVED KILLED SURVIVED`.
**The fault's semantics contribute zero bits.**

**(b) `arm A KILLED` on `:fake` was entailed, and the artifact says so in
English.** Arms A and C declare no `fake =` key, and every run writes
`"wiring_notes": ["… NO FAKE DECLARED, so --wiring fake ran its REAL adapter.
This column decides nothing about a fake for this port because there is not
one."]`. **`divergence.py`, `run_arm_swap.py` and `FI-04.md` read `wiring_notes`
nowhere.** The single reported divergence is *arm A's real run against arm B's
fake run*.

**(c) Re-anchor one call frame up and it vanishes.** FI-M18's
`re_anchoring_rule` says *"seed at whatever expression returns the durable
ledger's records to a reader"*. Applying **arm A's own rule** to arm B names
`Ledger.ledger_lines()` in `domain.py` — the method every arm exposes, and the
exact site arm C's FI-M19 already occupies. Seeding there gives `KILLED` on every
column of every arm and `NO DIVERGENCE MEASURED`, with arm B's measured
composition count dropping to **1**. **Same sentence, same arms, same corpus,
same driver.**

**(d) The selector was added after the seal.** `wired_by_default` does not exist
at `4697687`; both values were added at `e074ae5`, the commit that first ships
the results. Flipping it over the same unmodified runs gives **3 divergences on
different columns** and turns `corpus-port-swap:fake` **RED**. `FI-06-DF-09`.
*The declaration is currently TRUE and FI-06 verified it rather than inheriting
it* — `arms/arm_b/quota_ledger/__init__.py` returns `Ledger(quotas,
FileJournal(ledger_path))`. What does not exist is anything keeping it true.

**(e) The sealed analysis goes RED on the same data.** `divergence.py` at
`4697687` reports 4 divergences and exits **1** with `reachability CLAIMED and
NOT DEMONSTRATED on: suite-fake`. The shipped version splits that branch into
`REACHABLE_BY_ABSENCE`, which requires no demonstration and **cannot be red**.
`FI-04.md` says *"The assertion was not weakened"*; on that branch it was, in the
commit that shipped the results.

**(f) Three of five columns cannot be contradicted by any run.**
`divergence.py:114-124` hardcodes `distinct_compositions: 1` for every column
whose name does not end `:fake`. The file's own docstring warns that *"a
reachability analysis that cannot come out red is the fifth unfalsifiable
instrument in this repository"*. **Three fifths of its output is exactly that.**
And `corpus-action-bound` is a **reprint** of `corpus-port-swap:real` — evidence
blocks identical on every row of every arm — so the three-arm table has 13
column-slots and **8 distinct programs**. `FI-06-DF-10`.

## Why the verdict is still MET

The target names two acceptable outcomes and forbids exactly one — *"reporting
MISSED a second time without deciding which"*. FI-04 delivered **both**: an
explicit, reasoned retirement of the metric, and a demonstrated divergence that
reproduces from a regenerated corpus with green controls read out of the
artifacts.

**Moving the verdict to MISSED on the strength of an entailment argument found
afterwards would be editing a target to match a result** — in the direction that
looks more rigorous, which is the direction this project is most at risk from.
The verdict is MET and the entailment sits beside it at full strength.

**What was bought is one bit, and the bit is real.** `:real` KILLED on all three
arms was *not* entailed: the fault could have been invisible to the port corpus,
in which case the run goes `CLAIMED_REACHABLE_BUT_UNDEMONSTRATED` and red. And
arm B genuinely has a swappable seam that arms A and C do not, which is a
measured outcome of the prompts — arm C's author considered the seam and
**declined it on merit**.

**What was not bought is a measurement.** Given that one bit, every remaining
cell is derivable by reading three mapping files and one import graph with
nothing executed. **The retired metric entailed the null; the replacement entails
the positive.** That is an improvement — a fixture that can only say "no" is
worse than one that can say "yes" — and it is not a fixture whose arms could have
surprised us.

**And the one divergence points AGAINST the port**, which the catalogue wrote
down before it ran: *"swapping in the arm's own fake made a real durable fault
invisible, and no instrument reported that it had … it is a COST of the port, not
a benefit."*
