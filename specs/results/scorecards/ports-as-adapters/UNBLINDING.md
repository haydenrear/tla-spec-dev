The labels are drawn from a pool that excludes every label any prior round published (HP-06 used `X`/`Y`, EVAL-RERUN used `P`/`Q`), so a judge who stumbles into a sealed run cannot read this round's arms off it.

## What each judge could and could not see

**Each judge received:** all three artifacts' complete trees, sanitised, under
`blind/artifact_{T,U,W}/` — implementation, that author's own tests, that
author's `NOTES.md`; each artifact's `EVIDENCE.md` packet (shared-suite result,
full per-mutant per-instrument kill table with the `seeded_by` column, the
per-class block, the port-binding columns, the executability table, the control
status unabridged including every red one, and the mechanical block for **all
three** artifacts neutrally labelled); plus `references/eval_scorecard.md`, the
shared `FEATURE.md`, and the shared behavioral suite.

**A change from EVAL-RERUN, stated because it weakens one thing and strengthens
another.** That round gave each judge ONE artifact's source. This round gave each
judge all three, because three arms and two judges is six cards and a per-artifact
dispatch would have cost six agents with no cross-artifact reasoning available to
any of them. The cost is that a judge can compare sources; both judges disclosed
that they did, and both named exactly where it changed a score (the D4
discrimination). **The mechanical block was already cross-artifact in EVAL-RERUN
for the same reason — D2 anchor 3 reads a before/after that one column cannot
supply.**

**Each judge was forbidden:** either arm prompt, `arm_c/`,
`examples/validation/ab/README.md`, `seeded_faults.toml`, `check_catalogue.py`,
`PREDICTIONS-PA.md`, `PREDICTIONS-HP.md`, `reference/`, `reference_ports/`,
everything under `specs/results/scorecards/` except the three
`blind/artifact_*/` directories, everything under `specs/.history/`, every
`*EPIC*.md`, `specs/desired_program_model/`, `measure/`, `arms/`, this file, and
any other judge's output. The two judges were dispatched independently and
neither could see the other's work.

### The sanitising pass

`measure/make_blind_copies.py`. Case-insensitive substitutions over every `.py`
and `.md` in the copies, covering arm names, worktree and scratch paths,
`specs/results/scorecards/...` paths, ticket ids, `EVAL-RERUN`, `EVAL-SUPPRESS`,
`hexagonal` and `treatment`; then a second, independent case-insensitive grep for
the same vocabulary, which reported **clean**. Every `.py` was proved
**AST-identical** to its source with docstrings blanked, so nothing but comments
and docstrings changed.

**`REJECTED.md` was WITHHELD from the blind copies, and the asymmetry is the
reason.** PA-06 asked the one arm it dispatched itself for a rejected-designs
file; the two predecessor arms were never asked. Shipping it in one copy and not
the others would have told a judge which artifact this round produced — a bigger
leak than anything the file contains. It is read in the findings-by-channel
section instead, with its provenance stated.

### What still leaks, and no pass can fix it

**The blinding leaked through the NOTES, and both judges found it and said so
unprompted.** Artifact `T`'s `NOTES.md` quotes its own prompt and calls it "the
architecture ask"; `W`'s refers to "Section 1's instructions" with entirely
different content and to "Section 6's forbidden list"; `U`'s refers to no
sections at all. Both judges inferred from this that `T` had been given an
explicit ports-and-adapters instruction, and one recorded forming the inference
*before* finishing `T`'s D3.

Both stated the same conclusion in almost the same words: **the instruction to
read `NOTES.md` in full is in tension with the instruction to be blind to arm.**
This is unchanged from HP-06 and EVAL-RERUN, which recorded the same leak from
the same cause — an artifact whose author was asked to explain its design
describes that design. Any future round claiming a blind judgement of an
architecture prompt has to state it.

**Neither judge let it decide a score, and both said what their scores actually
rested on:** `T`'s D3 = 4 on a swap each judge executed themselves (one edited
the composition point in a scratch copy, the other wrote an external shim
constructing the domain over the fake and ran the entire unmodified shared
suite through it — 28 passed, no domain byte changed), and `W`'s D3 = 1 on a
probe that returned "no substitutable name exists".

### Disclosures both judges volunteered, none concealed, none re-run

1. **Both judges' first command included an `ls` of the repository root**, which
   printed the *names* of `PORTS-AS-ADAPTERS-EPIC.md`, `NEXT-EPIC.md`, three
   other `*EPIC*.md` files, `PORTS-AS-ADAPTERS-STARTER-PROMPT.md` and a
   `prompts/` directory. Names seen; nothing opened. This is the harness leaking
   through `ls`, the same disclosure EVAL-RERUN recorded, and the fix is not a
   judge's to make.
2. **Both judges compared artifacts**, deliberately, and both named the two
   places it entered their reasoning: the D4 discrimination (which artifact's own
   suite fails under one shared mutation) and the verdicts.
3. **Both judges seeded their own faults and ran them** rather than scoring the
   packet — seven and one respectively, applied in semantically matched form to
   all three artifacts. Both said, unprompted, that this changed a score.

**None was re-run.** Discarding a card after seeing its score is the one move a
round may not make.

### The third-pass rule fired zero times, for the third round running

| artifact | D1 | D2 | D3 | D4 | D5 | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `T` (arm B) | 4 / 3 | 2 / 2 | **4 / 4** | 4 / 4 | 4 / 4 | 18 / 17 |
| `U` (arm A) | 4 / 3 | 2 / 2 | 2 / 2 | 4 / 4 | 4 / 4 | 16 / 15 |
| `W` (arm C) | 3 / 3 | 2 / 2 | **1 / 1** | 3 / 3 | 4 / 4 | 13 / 13 |

Maximum spread across all thirty independent scores is **1**, on D1 for `T` and
`U`. **Zero dimensions are contested and no third pass was run.** Twelve of the
fifteen dimension pairs agreed exactly.

Agreement this tight is not on its own a virtue, and the sealed confound says
why: two judges of the same model family reading the same anchors are not
independent the way two people would be. Both ran on `claude-opus-5[1m]`.

**What does argue for the protocol:** both judges independently refused to count
the same piece of evidence — `T`'s diverging `corpus-port-swap:fake` cell — as
D3 evidence, one because the column sits under a red control and one because it
preferred a swap it could see fail. Both independently declined a `D3 = 3` for
`U` after a monkeypatch probe succeeded. And both independently arrived at the
same criticism of the round: **that the apparatus separates three materially
different artifacts by three cells out of ninety-nine.**
