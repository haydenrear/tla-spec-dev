      requirement more fully; shrinking the reason list would be the wrong move
      and is stated as such in the docstring.

      ANSWER rather than an absence. The movement line still yields nothing --
      the test subject was never in question -- and the counted line is now
      asserted to be UNREACHABLE with its reason named. A test that asserts a
      whole file yields nothing goes red whenever the recogniser learns anything,
      correctly or not.
