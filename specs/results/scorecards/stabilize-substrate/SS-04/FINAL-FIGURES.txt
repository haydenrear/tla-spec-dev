SS-04 -- THE AUTHORITATIVE FIGURES, AND WHY THEY ARE IN A .txt
==============================================================================

This file is `.txt`. `DEFAULT_SWEEP` reads `*.md`, `references/*.md`,
`specs/results/scorecards/**/*.md`, one `.toml`, one `.json`, one `.yaml` and
one `.md` under `specs/results/` -- IT DOES NOT READ THIS. That is the whole
reason the numbers live here: `RESULT.md` is inside the corpus it measures, so
writing a counted figure into it CHANGES the count it is reporting. This ticket
watched that happen four times. Here a figure can be written down without
becoming one.

`SS-01-DF-03`: a figure is a joint property of the artifact AND THE TREE IT WAS
MEASURED IN. Every number below names its tree.

  AND THE LARGEST MISS IS IN NO SUPERSET AT ALL: a counted figure with no
  `<n> of <m>` in it. `8 failed, 1490 passed` is two counts and a denominator
  that must be added; `seven epics, zero bugs` is a numerator over a population
  named nowhere; `four rounds' claims` has no denominator. Reaching those means
  INVENTING the population.

  PRECISION, HAND-AUDITED: 38 of 40 deterministically sampled matches
  (random.seed(277)) are genuine counted figures, adjudicated one at a time
  against their source lines. The two failures are one class, the
  partitive/distributive `one of the N`; the distributive half is repaired and
  pinned, the partitive half is filed as SS-04-DF-02 and NOT patched.
