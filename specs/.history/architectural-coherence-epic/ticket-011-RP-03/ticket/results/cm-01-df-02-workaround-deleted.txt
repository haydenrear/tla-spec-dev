RP-03 -- owner-authorized deletion of the CM-01-DF-02 workaround
================================================================

WHAT WAS DELETED
----------------
scripts/spec_evolution.py:
  * CFG_KEYWORDS (a 19-name frozenset of TLC config keywords), formerly at
    line 539;
  * the `if invariant in CFG_KEYWORDS: continue` skip in the model-pair
    validator, formerly at lines 745-753.

Both existed only because analyze_complexity.parse_cfg_invariants used to hand
back the bare keyword line that ENDS an INVARIANT block ("INVARIANT Inv" followed
by a bare "CONSTANTS") as if it were another invariant name, which would have
manufactured a mismatch on a .tla/.cfg pair that matches. RP-04 fixed the parser.

WHY IT IS SAFE
--------------
RP-04's measurement, at
specs/.history/architectural-coherence-epic/ticket-009-RP-04/ticket/results/cm-01-df-02-workaround-is-now-dead.txt

  EXPERIMENT A -- workaround removed, RP-04 parser fix present:
    1 failed, 897 passed  (the one failure was the then-known-red
    test_skill_requires_two_minute_case_generation_budget, since fixed by RP-05)
  EXPERIMENT B -- workaround removed, PRE-FIX parser:
    4 failed, 857 passed  (three model-pair validator tests fail without it)

RP-04 left it in place because scripts/spec_evolution.py is CM-01's conflict key
and no open ticket owned it. The epic owner assigned the deletion to RP-03.
