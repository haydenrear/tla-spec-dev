THE REPRODUCTION CONTROL, and it is why map-none is in the table at all.
HP-03's `corpus-whole` column, over the same catalogue and the same reference:

  M01 S  M02 S  M03 S  M04 S  M05 K  M06 K  M07 S  M08 K  M09 K  M10 K

map-none reproduces it cell for cell, all ten. The seam this instrument
installs -- routing the durable append through the port while still writing the
file -- did not change the baseline, so the map-checking column can be read as a
mapping effect and nothing else.

THE RESULT, stated narrowly.
  * The mapping choice is worth EXACTLY ONE CELL on this catalogue: M04, and
    only M04. map-checking and map-silent are otherwise identical in all ten
    rows.
  * durable_content moves 1 of 2 -> 2 of 2 under the checking mapping. That is
    the third independent reproduction of the checking/silent split (round 1
    and round 2 at 2 of 6 vs 4 of 6 on ex4; a blind agent at 3 of 3 vs 0 of 3
    on a fresh 16-mutant catalogue) and the first on this fixture.
  * The mapping cost nothing anywhere: map-checking >= map-none in every one of
    the ten rows. The guard on GOAL-catch-bugs holds.

AND THE PART THAT IS UNFLATTERING TO THE HEADLINE.

M05 WAS ALREADY A CORPUS KILL. The dispatch brief said M04 and M05 "both
currently survive both corpora and die only on the hand-written suite." That is
true of M04 and false of M05: HP-03's own committed kill table records M05 as
KILLED by corpus-whole, and this run reproduces it under all three mappings
including map-none. The reason is in the model -- the CLOSE element is
<< "CLOSE", t, committed[t] >>, so its third slot IS the total and the ordinary
projected-state comparison already sees a zeroed one. Only the COMMIT element
(<< "COMMIT", holder[r], amt[r] >>) drops the total, and only M04 lives there.
So the durable_content row was 1 of 2 before this ticket, not 0 of 2, and the
honest claim is ONE mutant moved, not two.

M07 STILL SURVIVES EVERY CORPUS INSTRUMENT. It is the catalogue's declared
POSITIVE CONTROL -- it is supposed to die everywhere -- and it does not,
because it is seeded inside reserve() and Reserve contributes zero executable
cases (HP-03 measured this and filed it). Nothing in HP-05 touches that, and by
the catalogue's own P05 doctrine the wrong_value row is not citeable as a clean
kill measurement while its control is red.

GUARD RELAXATION IS 0 of 3 IN EVERY COLUMN HERE, including map-checking. That
is expected and is not a regression: the whole-view corpus contains no rejected
input at all, and the class is HP-03's negative corpus's, not this ticket's.
